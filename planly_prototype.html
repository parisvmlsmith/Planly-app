About Planly

A simpler way to plan

This is an early, free proof of concept — not a connected messaging service. It demonstrates the user journey while we validate demand.

What works today

Paste text, detect likely plans with basic rules, edit details, save events locally and export .ics calendar files.

What comes later

Real AI understanding, account sync, permission-based integrations, shared plans and messaging — subject to technical feasibility and user demand.
